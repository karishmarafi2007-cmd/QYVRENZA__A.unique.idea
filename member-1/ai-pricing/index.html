03 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

Still have a question? The details here should help you decide.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move between Explorer and Studio as your needs change. Company plans are arranged with your team.

What counts as an assisted action?

An assisted action is a request you make to an AI feature in your workspace. The exact usage is shown alongside the feature before you use it.

How does yearly billing work?

Studio is $19 per month when billed annually, charged as a single $228 payment for the year. The billing switch updates the displayed amount and total.

Can I try the product before paying?

Explorer is free and does not require a payment card. It includes a small set of projects and assisted actions so you can explore the workspace.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No card is needed for Explorer. Any paid-plan checkout would be handled by the product when a real billing service is connected.
